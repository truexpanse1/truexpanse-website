The Business Growth Checklist

7 Levers That Grow Your Business

The simple moves growing entrepreneurs and small-to-medium businesses use to win more customers, raise their profit, and scale — without working more hours.

You don't have to work harder to grow. You need a few simple systems working for you. These are seven of them — the levers that turn the effort you're already putting in into real, repeatable growth. Read down the list, pick the one that would move your business the most this month, and put it to work first.

1

Win more of the leads you already get

The fastest growth isn't always more leads — it's turning more of the ones already coming in into paying customers. People buy from whoever shows up first and stays in touch. A quick, consistent follow-up is the single highest-return move most businesses are leaving on the table.

Do thisCapture every lead the moment it comes in, and set one rule: reply within 24 hours, and follow up at least three times. Showing up beats being the cheapest.
2

Run your business by the numbers

Growing businesses know their score. When you can see — at a glance — your leads, your quotes, and your closes, you know exactly what to do more of to hit your goal. Clear targets turn a good week into a repeatable system, and give your team a number to chase.

Do thisPick three numbers that drive your growth — leads in, quotes out, deals won — and look at them every day. A number you watch is a number that climbs.
3

Price for the value you deliver

Healthy growth needs healthy margins. When your price reflects the real value of your work — and covers your true costs — every new customer makes you stronger, not just busier. Confident pricing funds better people, better tools, and the room to scale.

Do thisBuild one simple cost sheet so you know what each job or sale truly costs. Then price for profit and the value you bring — not to be the cheapest option.
4

Make every marketing dollar pull its weight

The businesses that scale know exactly which efforts bring customers — so they pour fuel on what works and stop guessing. You don't need a bigger budget to grow. You need to know which dollar is winning, then do more of that.

Do thisAsk every new customer "How did you hear about us?" and write it down. In 30 days you'll see exactly where to invest more — and grow faster on the same spend.
5

Win the deal by being first

A ready buyer rewards speed. When your quote or reply lands the same day while everyone else takes three, you win — even at a higher price. Being fast and clear is a competitive edge you can turn on this week.

Do thisBuild a quote-and-reply template you can send fast, and aim to respond the same day every time. Fast and clear closes more than slow and perfect.
6

Build systems so your business grows beyond you

Real scale starts the day the business can run without you in every seat. When your best ways of working live in simple, repeatable systems, you can hire with confidence, serve more customers, and finally step up to lead instead of doing it all yourself.

Do thisWrite down how you do the things you do most — how you quote, deliver, and follow up. One checklist at a time turns what's in your head into a business someone else can run.
7

Grow from the customers you already have

Your warmest growth is sitting in your customer list. The people who already trust you are the easiest to sell again — and the most likely to refer you. A simple habit of staying in touch turns one-time buyers into repeat customers and a referral engine.

Do thisKeep a list of every customer and reach out on a schedule — a check-in, a helpful tip, a simple "who else can we help?" The relationship is where the next sale lives.
