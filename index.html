How can my business increase revenue?+
Most owners try to grow by working more hours. There is a better way: aim at the right targets, put marketing ahead of sales, and build a system that brings work to you. The free strategy session includes a quick assessment that shows the few levers that will move your revenue the most — based on your numbers, not generic advice.
Why is marketing senior to sales?+
Because you cannot sell to people who do not know you exist. Marketing fills the room; sales closes the people in it. Most owners pour everything into selling and starve the marketing that feeds it, then wonder why leads are thin. This is one of the core principles Don teaches as a licensed 10X Business Coach, drawn from Grant Cardone's training, and he shows you how it applies to your business in plain terms on your strategy session.
