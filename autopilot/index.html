Questions, answered

Do I really get the website for free?

Yes. We build and host your landing page as part of your subscription — there's no separate build fee. It stays live and maintained for as long as you're a member.

Why a 6-month foundation?

Marketing compounds. The first weeks build the asset; the real momentum — consistent content, a tuned offer, posts that prove themselves — shows up over a few months. Six months lets the system actually work. After that, you're month-to-month.

What do I have to do?

Almost nothing. We may ask for a few photos of your work and a quick intro call to capture your voice and offer. Then we run it. You stay on the tools.

Who writes the posts and makes the images?

We do — using AI tuned to your trade and your voice, then reviewed before anything goes out. They're on-brand and built to get attention, not generic filler.

What happens after the 6 months?

You continue month-to-month at the same plan price, or stop anytime. No long-term contract beyond the foundation.
