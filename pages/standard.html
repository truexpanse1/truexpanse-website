The 10 Compliance Domains

What Every Site Must Prove
Before It Earns Our Name

Each domain has a Foundation (must-pass) tier, a Professional tier, and a Premium tier. Foundation failures block launch. Professional is our default for client builds. Premium is what truexpanse.com itself targets.

Domain 1

Technical Foundation

Site is reachable, served safely, and deployed from git — not dropped from a folder. SSL, HSTS, no broken links, no 404s on assets. Continuous deploy from GitHub on every push.

Domain 2

SEO Fundamentals

Unique title, meta description, canonical, single H1 per page. Open Graph + Twitter Cards. Sitemap with current lastmod dates. Robots.txt explicitly allowing every AI agent.

Domain 3 · TASRS

AI-Search Readiness

100-point rubric across 6 pillars: Structured Data (25), Content Clarity (20), AI Bot Accessibility (15), Freshness (10), Brand Signals (15), Live AI Visibility (15). Target: 85+ Pro, 95+ Premium.

Domain 4

Performance

Core Web Vitals pass at 75th percentile. LCP under 2.5s, CLS under 0.1, INP under 200ms. Lighthouse mobile 80+. Image budgets enforced. Fonts swap, not block.

Domain 5

Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Color contrast, keyboard navigation, focus states, form labels, semantic headings, screen-reader tested. Required by California law and the right thing to do.

Domain 6

Security & Privacy

HTTPS-only, full security header set, no client-side secrets, CCPA-compliant privacy policy. Cookie consent where required. Annual review against securityheaders.com.

Domain 7

Conversion & UX

Specific hero offer (not "Free Quote"), CTA above the fold, tap-to-call phone, trust signals visible without scroll, thank-you page that confirms next steps. Forms ≤ 5 fields on first ask.

Domain 8

Brand Integrity

Logo never stretched or recolored. One brand color for primary CTAs only. Two font families max. Owner photography in consistent style — no stock heroes. One icon family throughout.

Domain 9

Analytics & Lead Capture

GA4 receiving real-time data. Netlify forms with email + GHL webhook. Conversion events configured. UTM parameters preserved through forms. No lead falls through.

Domain 10

Content Quality

Real testimonials with specific results. Owner bio + photo. Service descriptions answer what / who / what's included / outcome. Reading level Grade 8 or below. Quarterly content refresh.

FAQ · About the Standard

Common Questions About How We Grade

What is the TrueXpanse Standard? +

A 10-domain compliance rubric every website TrueXpanse builds, hosts, or audits is graded against. Covers technical foundation, SEO, AI-search readiness (TASRS), performance, accessibility, security, conversion, brand integrity, analytics, and content quality. Foundation tier is must-pass for launch. Professional is our client default. Premium is showcase caliber.

What is TASRS? +

The TrueXpanse AI-Search Readiness Score — a 100-point rubric measuring how well a website is set up to be discovered and recommended by AI agents like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude. It covers six pillars and is Domain 3 of the broader Standard.

Why does AI-search readiness matter? +

Customers increasingly ask AI agents instead of typing into Google. When someone asks ChatGPT "who's the best painting contractor in Paso Robles?", the AI doesn't pick randomly — it picks businesses whose websites have explicit structured data, clear content, fresh updates, and strong brand signals. Sites that aren't optimized are functionally invisible in those conversations.

How do I know my current site is failing? +

Most contractor and small-business websites score below 60 on TASRS — that's an F. They're missing FAQ schema, AggregateRating, AI-bot allowlists, current dateModified, and review markup. Book a free strategy session and we'll grade your site live across all 10 domains.

Do you grade your own site? +

Yes, every quarter. Current truexpanse.com score is published above in the Live Scorecard section, including failing checks we're working on. Radical transparency is the point — you can see exactly how we score, what we're improving, and the standard we hold ourselves to.
