The Principles Behind Growing Your Business

A few of the ideas Don teaches owners to grow revenue, win attention, and generate leads — and what you walk away with from a free strategy session. No hype, no fine print.

How can my business increase revenue?

Most owners try to grow by working more hours. There's a better way: aim at the right targets, put marketing ahead of sales, and build a system that brings work to you. The free strategy session includes a quick assessment that shows the few levers that will move your revenue the most — based on your numbers, not generic advice.

Why is marketing "senior to" sales?

Because you can't sell to people who don't know you exist. Marketing fills the room; sales closes the people in it. Most owners pour everything into selling and starve the marketing that feeds it — then wonder why leads are thin. This is one of the core principles Don teaches as a licensed 10X Business Coach, drawn from Grant Cardone's training, and he shows you how it applies to your business in plain terms on your strategy session.

What's the fastest way to win more attention for my business?

Attention is the first thing you earn — before anyone buys, they have to notice you. The fastest wins are usually showing up where your customers already look, posting consistently, and making one clear offer instead of five. On your strategy session, Don shows you which attention move will pay off fastest for your specific business.

How do I set targets that actually move the needle?

A goal you can't measure is just a wish. Don coaches owners to set targets you can track every day — calls, leads, jobs, and revenue — and to take massive action toward them. That's the idea behind his Massive Action Tracker app: know your number, hit it daily, and the year takes care of itself. Your strategy session maps the right targets for you.

How do I generate more leads without chasing them?

Chasing leads is exhausting and it doesn't scale. The goal is to attract them — with an offer people actually want, content that builds trust, and follow-up that never drops the ball. Set up right, leads come to you and stay with you. On your session, Don shows you the one gap costing you the most leads right now.
