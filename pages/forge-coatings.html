The Three-Layer System

Why most coatings fail.
Why ours doesn't.

01

Polyurea Basecoat

After diamond-grinding the slab, our polyurea base coat mechanically locks into the concrete pores. Fast-curing — chosen by temperature on install day. The bond is the foundation. Skip this, and everything fails.

02

Decorative Chip Broadcast

¼" vinyl chips broadcast into the wet polyurea, to refusal in full-broadcast applications. 33 standard blends plus custom color matching. Hides imperfections, adds slip resistance, gives every floor its character.

03

Polyaspartic HS Topcoat

Up to 16 mils thick in a single pass. UV-stable, chemical-resistant, abrasion-resistant. The surface you actually walk on, drive on, and live on. The reason your floor still looks new years later.
