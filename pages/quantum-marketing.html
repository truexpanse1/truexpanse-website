The Problem

Most agencies sell you ads.
That's why most ad budgets get burned.

Ads alone are not a system. Without a clear goal, a measurable target, the right keywords, a landing page that converts, a lead magnet worth trading an email for, a CRM that holds the lead, and a nurture sequence that turns the lead into a sale — you are paying Google to teach you what doesn't work. We refuse to start there.

The North Star

It always starts with the goal —
never with the tactic.

Three layers, in this order. Get them right, and every dollar after is leveraged. Get them wrong, and no campaign can save you.

01

The Goal

Your business outcome. Not a vanity metric — the number that, if hit, materially changes your year.

e.g. "$500K added revenue"
02

The Target

The specific monthly milestone that, repeated, gets you to the goal. The thing we plan, build, and report against every week.

e.g. "100 qualified leads / mo"
03

The KPIs

The instruments on the dashboard. They tell you, every week, how close you are to the target — so course corrections happen before the budget runs dry, not after.

CPL · CPA · Conv % · LTV/CAC
The Engine

Nine moving parts.
One job: get the right person to raise their hand.

Skip a step and the next one breaks. This is why DIY ads, freelancer ads, and "we'll figure it out" ads almost always fail — the pieces don't connect.

01

Keywords

What your ideal buyer actually types into Google when their problem is at its loudest.

Why it mattersWrong keywords = wrong audience. Even brilliant ads can't save you if the people seeing them don't have the problem you solve.
02

Headlines

Big promises in 30 characters or less. We write 12–15 per ad group and let Google A/B them in real time.

Why it mattersThe headline decides whether the click happens. Higher click-through means lower cost per click — Google rewards relevance with cheaper traffic.
03

Descriptions

Specificity, social proof, offer stack, risk reversal, urgency — all in 90 characters. The Sabri Suby Godfather framework.

Why it mattersDescriptions disqualify the wrong-fit clicks (saving budget) and give the right-fit clicks the confidence to act.
04

Landing Page

One page per service. No nav, no distractions. Built around the exact promise the ad just made.

Why it mattersSending paid traffic to a homepage is the fastest way to waste budget. Every click deserves a page that picks up where the ad left off.
05

The Lead Magnet

A high-value piece of content the visitor wants enough to trade their email for. Solves a real problem they're researching right now.

Why it matters80% of visitors aren't ready to buy on visit one. The lead magnet captures them and gives us a way to keep talking.
06

Email Capture

One click. The guide opens instantly. Their name, email, and source are captured the same second.

Why it mattersEvery email captured is a future revenue line. The ad got us one shot. The email gets us a hundred.
07

CRM (GoHighLevel)

Every lead lands in your CRM, automatically tagged by source, service interest, and lead magnet.

Why it mattersWithout a tagged CRM, leads disappear into an inbox and rot. With one, you sort, filter, and act on the highest-intent leads first.
08

Nurture

An email sequence that builds trust over 30 days. Not pitches — useful answers to the questions they're actually asking.

Why it mattersBuying decisions take time. The competitor who shows up helpfully for 30 days wins the sale — even if the lead originally intended to buy elsewhere.
09

The Close

By the time the lead picks up the phone, they already trust you. The conversation is about logistics, not persuasion.

Why it mattersClosing rate triples when nurture has done the work. Less time per close = more closes per week at the same ad budget.
