The Three Clocks — on every page of the MAT, all day long.

Every sales leader knows this feeling.

Your reps are spread across the country. The CRM says everyone is busy. The pipeline says otherwise. When the quarter misses, everybody has a story — and you have no way to check it.

You can't coach what you can't see. Activity reports tell you what got logged. They don't tell you where the hours went — and hours are what you're paying for.

We built the answer into the Massive Action Tracker, and it runs on one rule: tell the truth.

The Three Clocks

Every page in the MAT belongs to one of three buckets. While a rep actively works, the matching clock runs — visible at the top of every screen.

Marketing

Time spent creating and pushing the message out — content, images, campaigns, social. The work that fills the top of the funnel.

Sales

Time working leads — call lists, dials, dispositions, hot leads, pipeline. Every touch on a deal, tied to real logged outcomes.

Execution

Time on follow-through — planning, appointments, targets, programs, and the end-of-day report. Where promises become results.

Idle — the truth column

Two minutes with no clicks, typing, or scrolling and the clock stops. That time is recorded as idle — not hidden, not padded onto anything. Eight hours logged in doing nothing shows up as exactly that.

At the end of the day, AI writes the accountability report.

Every rep's end-of-day report shows where their time went. Click any category and the AI summarizes what was actually done with those hours — built strictly from the day's logged activity: dials made, outcomes, appointments.

It has standing orders: never flatter, never invent. If a rep made 40 dials and booked 3 appointments, it says so. If the sales clock shows 20 minutes, it says that too — plainly, with one concrete next step.

That's not surveillance. That's a mirror — and the reps who want to win love the mirror.

Straight answers

Can a rep game it by leaving the app open?

No. Two minutes without input and the clock stops — that time lands in the idle column, visible to the rep and on their end-of-day report. The system's one rule is to tell the truth.

Is this spyware on my reps' computers?

No. It tracks time inside the MAT platform only — the place their sales work already lives. No screen recording, no keystroke logging, no tracking outside the app. Reps see their own clocks all day; nothing is hidden from them.
