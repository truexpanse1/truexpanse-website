Questions you probably have

How long does it take to generate a week of posts?

Most users generate a full week of platform-ready posts in under 20 minutes from the moment they sign in. Pick the topic, MAT writes the post and generates the image, you review and edit if needed, and publish.

Do I need a designer or copywriter?

No. MAT writes platform-specific captions for Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn — each formatted to its own conventions and length — and generates a custom image for each one. Publish-ready out of the box. You only edit when you want to add your personal voice.

What platforms can I publish to?

Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn directly via the built-in GoHighLevel social planner integration. You can also export any post — download the image and copy the caption — for use in Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, or your platform of choice.

Can I edit what the AI generates?

Yes. Every post is fully editable before publishing — caption text, image, hashtags, CTA, scheduled time. The AI gives you a strong starting point; you keep full creative control.

How is this different from Buffer, Hootsuite, or Canva?

Buffer and Hootsuite are schedulers — you still have to write the post and design the image. Canva is a design tool — you still have to think of what to post. MAT generates the post idea, writes the caption tuned to the platform, creates the image, and publishes it. One platform, one workflow, one price.
